Some heads allocate roughly equal attention mass to every doubling of distance. That corresponds to approximately inverse-distance weight per key. It is a measured pattern for a subset of heads, not a law imposed on every RoPE head.

A matched-history measurement

The investigation measured native dense attention in Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, across all 336 query heads. It sampled the final 128 scored queries in each of four roughly 2k-token prose excerpts. Every sampled query had every tested age available. This avoids confusing fewer available distant keys with lower weight per key.

The fit uses seven complete bins: 8–15, 16–31, 32–63, 64–127, 128–255, 256–511, and 512–1023. Self-attention, the nearest tokens, and the far incomplete edge are excluded. Within each bin, attention mass is divided by the number of available keys before fitting.

Approximate inverse-distance behavior was defined descriptively as exponent \(\lambda\in[0.8,1.2]\), log-space \(R^2\ge0.8\), positive mass in all bins, and at least 1% of total attention in the fitted range. By that criterion, 101 of 333 eligible dense heads matched: 30.33%. Only 46 met the criterion separately in every passage. Across individual eligible query/head cases, the matching fraction was 15.82%.

The pooled median exponent was 0.97295, with broad variation between heads. Raising the range-mass cutoff to 10% left 88 matches among 292 eligible heads. These thresholds describe the archive; they are not a formal statistical test distinguishing a power law from alternative distributions.

Mass per octave versus weight per key

Let \(w(m)\) be the average attention weight per key at positive age \(m\). If each multiplicative interval \([a,2a]\) receives the same mass, then in a smooth idealization

\[w(m)\,\mathrm dm=C\,\mathrm d(\log m)=\frac{C}{m}\,\mathrm dm,\qquad w(m)=\frac C m.\]

Twice as many keys share the same budget in the next octave. For a general power law, \(w(m)\propto m^{-\lambda}\), octave mass scales as \(a^{1-\lambda}\); it is constant when \(\lambda=1\). On discrete integer bins, equal octave mass and exact \(1/m\) weights coincide only approximately.

Thus “attention mass decreases as 1/m” can be misleading. Per-key weights can decrease while a whole distance octave keeps roughly constant mass. One key per age is assumed here; other sampling layouts require their own exposure counts.

A conditional maximum-entropy derivation

On finite support \(m=1,\ldots,M\), assume normalization and a fixed expected log-age, \(\sum_m p_m\log m=\mu\). Maximizing Shannon entropy gives

\[\mathcal L=-\sum_m p_m\log p_m-a(\sum_m p_m-1)-\lambda(\sum_m p_m\log m-\mu).\] \[\partial_{p_m}\mathcal L=-\log p_m-1-a-\lambda\log m=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p_m=\frac{m^{-\lambda}}{\sum_{r=1}^M r^{-\lambda}}.\]

Strict concavity makes this the unique maximum for an interior feasible constraint. The constraint determines \(\lambda\); it does not force \(\lambda=1\). At one, normalization is \(p_m=1/(mH_M)\). An exact inverse-distance distribution cannot be normalized on an infinite positive-integer range.

The earlier discussion cited this Zenodo record, which could not be retrieved during this verification. The author’s accessible How Transformers Attend chapter on decay explicitly treats the log-distance constraint as a hypothesis. The derivation above can be checked independently; neither maximum entropy nor a fixed log-distance budget has been established as the training mechanism of our Qwen heads.

Where geometric RoPE frequencies enter—and where the proof stops

Geometrically spaced wavelengths give a ladder of logarithmic distance scales. As distance grows, progressively more frequency terms oscillate rapidly. With suitable learned alignment, amplitudes, and averaging, a roughly constant score penalty per doubling is plausible. The missing step is showing that actual learned coefficients and content statistics satisfy those conditions.

Softmax supplies an exact conversion if logits already have the right shape:

\[s(m)=c-\lambda\log m\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p_m\propto m^{-\lambda}\quad(T=1).\]

Equal logits instead give uniform attention. A single RoPE sinusoid is oscillatory and can peak far from zero; finite sums need not decrease monotonically. Geometric spacing alone therefore cannot establish inverse-distance attention or fix its exponent.

Barbero et al. directly challenge universal decay claims. Loveland et al., Formalizing and Mitigating Structural Distortion in LLM Attention for Zero-Shot Graph Reasoning analyze decay under explicit alignment/linearization assumptions. Such assumptions belong in the claim, not in a footnote dropped when applying it to a trained model.

Our archive supports an averaged decay pattern on specified texts and heads. A causal explanation would additionally need interventions on frequency contributions, content-preserving controls, and fresh evaluation. A good log-log fit alone does not identify the mechanism.
