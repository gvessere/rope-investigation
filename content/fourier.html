RoPE scores are sums of sinusoids. That makes Fourier analysis a useful lens—but does not make the standard RoPE frequency set a complete DFT basis.

What the DFT guarantees

\[F_k=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}f_n e^{-2\pi i kn/N},\qquad f_n=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}F_k e^{2\pi i kn/N}.\]

For any finite sequence of \(N\) samples, the full DFT reconstructs those samples up to numerical precision. A real sequence has conjugate symmetry, giving \(N\) independent real degrees of freedom—not \(N\) unrestricted complex ones. Reconstruction at those points says nothing about the unique continuous function between them. The usual Fourier continuation is periodic.

A truncated basis has fewer degrees of freedom and cannot reproduce every sampled target. RoPE adds another restriction: its geometrically spaced frequencies are generally not the equally spaced DFT harmonics of the selected sample window. For \(p\) frequency pairs there are at most \(2p\) real coefficient degrees of freedom.

To fit a target at distances \(\Delta_t\), form the matrix

\[X_{t,:}=\left[\cos(\omega_0\Delta_t),\ldots,\cos(\omega_{p-1}\Delta_t),\sin(\omega_0\Delta_t),\ldots,\sin(\omega_{p-1}\Delta_t)\right].\]

Least squares chooses coefficients \(c\) to minimize \(\|Xc-f\|_2\). Exact interpolation requires that the target lie in the matrix’s column space; arbitrary interpolation requires full row rank. Nearby low frequencies can produce nearly dependent columns over a short interval. Formal coefficient count alone is insufficient: singular values, conditioning, coefficient norm, and precision matter.

Positionally similar does not mean semantically redundant

Two nearby slow frequencies may have almost indistinguishable rotation slopes on a short window. Their independent learned projections can still respond to different content. Redundancy of positional basis columns therefore does not establish that the associated dimensions are zeroed out, interchangeable backups, or safe to prune.

Barbero et al., Round and Round We Go! investigate trained positional patterns and low-frequency use in Gemma. Their semantic interpretation of low-frequency use is a hypothesis; it should not be treated as proof about every head in the Qwen experiments here.
